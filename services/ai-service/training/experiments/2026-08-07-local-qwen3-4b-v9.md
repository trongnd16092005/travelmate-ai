# Qwen3 4B local v9 — natural conversation UX

## Goal

Improve multi-slot follow-ups, corrections, same-trip retention, clean trip switching, and
natural clarification without regressing itinerary, budget, checklist, or safety behavior.

## Data and training

- Continued fine-tune from `travelmate-qwen3-4b-lora-v8-state-transition`.
- Added 455 v9 examples across five UX categories.
- Training split: 905 records (305 new + 600 v8 replay).
- Validation split: 177 records (77 new + 100 v8 replay).
- Held-out UX test: 20 balanced cases; no prompt overlap with train/validation.
- One epoch, 57 optimizer steps, learning rate `1e-5`, LoRA rank 8.
- Train loss: 0.2192; validation loss: 0.2784.
- Validation mean token accuracy: 0.9363.

## Evaluation

The raw adapter still misses two natural-clarification variants and isolated long compound
answers remain its weakest category. The runtime state machine supplies deterministic
clarification and composes itinerary, budget, and checklist sections, so the production demo
does not rely on those weak raw paths.
