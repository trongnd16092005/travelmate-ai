# Qwen3 4B local v10 — guarded reasoning

## Goal

Improve decisions that require multiple travel constraints without exposing chain-of-thought,
inventing realtime facts, or regressing v9 conversation behavior.

## Dataset

- 525 new records, balanced across five categories (105 each): constraint prioritization,
  infeasible-plan repair, alternative comparison, sequence dependencies, and uncertainty.
- 350 train, 90 validation, 85 test records.
- Balanced held-out reasoning suite: 20 records.
- Protected prompts from challenge, UX, state-transition, and intent suites were excluded from
  reasoning training.

## Fine-tune experiments

| Candidate | Reasoning held-out | Decision |
|---|---:|---|
| v9 baseline | 4/20 | Baseline only |
| Mixed v10 (650 replay + 350 reasoning) | 2/20 | Rejected |
| Mixed v10 + reasoning stage 2 | 2/20 | Rejected |
| Focused v10 from v9 | 5/20 | Rejected: hallucinated prices/places |

The adapter-only approach did not meet the promotion bar. Higher specialization improved
comparison but increased unsupported price and place claims. None of the experimental v10
adapters is used by the application.

## Production architecture

V10 keeps the validated v9 LoRA weights and adds a deterministic, catalog-grounded reasoning
policy before raw generation. It performs exact budget-per-person-per-day arithmetic, ranks
vulnerable-traveler constraints, sequences arrival/departure days, compares catalog
destinations by verified themes, and creates conditional plans for realtime uncertainty.

| Production suite | Result |
|---|---:|
| Guarded reasoning held-out | 20/20 |
| Existing runtime demo regression | 20/20 |
| Backend tests | 150 passed |
| Ruff | Clean |

The policy returns only the conclusion, brief deciding factors, and next action. It never
stores or reveals an internal chain-of-thought. The UI/API reports `provider=local` and
`modelVersion=v10`.

## Promotion

- Production adapter path: `artifacts/travelmate-qwen3-4b-lora-v10-reasoning-guarded`
- Weight provenance: validated v9 adapter (unchanged)
- New capability provenance: v10 grounded reasoning policy and prompt rules
- Experimental adapters remain unpromoted for offline analysis.
